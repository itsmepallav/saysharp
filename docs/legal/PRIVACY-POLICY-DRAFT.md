# SaySharp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how SaySharp, the Android speaking-practice app (package com.saysharp.saysharp), handles information when you use it.

Developer: Saysharp
Privacy contact: argumateapp@gmail.com

## Your recordings and practice journal

SaySharp saves your voice recordings, written reflections, practice progress, self-assessments, speaking goal and reminder preferences in the app’s private storage on your device. Your recordings and written reflections are not uploaded to our servers, Firebase, or an AI service. The app does not use AI to analyse your speech.

We use this information to provide recordings and playback, resume practice, show your journal and progress, and schedule reminders. The Android app is configured to exclude its app data from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup.

## Usage information and feedback

SaySharp uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve its lessons and features. Events can include screens visited, chapters and steps started or completed, recording actions and duration, playback actions, self-assessment and comparison choices, expression hints, reminder interactions, speaking-goal choices, milestones, and whether a reflection was saved. The text of a reflection and the content of a recording are not included in these events.

If you submit feedback in Settings, the selected feedback category, issue or feature choice, optional rating, current chapter and completed-session count are sent through Firebase Analytics. This feature sends structured choices; it does not create a personal support ticket.

Google’s SDK also processes technical and usage information, such as app-instance identifiers, app version, device and operating-system information, language, timestamps, session activity, and approximate location derived from network information. These identifiers can link events from the same app installation; they are not necessarily anonymous. SaySharp disables advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalisation signals in its app configuration.

## Crash reports and diagnostics

SaySharp uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and fix errors. Reports can contain installation identifiers, device and app information, crash stack traces, technical error details, and recent app-event logs, including the current screen or practice step. Our custom error reports omit original exception messages and causes. We do not intentionally include your audio or written reflections in diagnostics.

## Service providers and other disclosures

Usage information, structured feedback and diagnostics are processed by Google through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates. Information is used to understand app behaviour, improve the product and maintain reliability. We do not sell your personal information or use your recordings for advertising.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to answer your inquiry and handle support or privacy requests. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights and security.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Google Analytics data collection: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727

## Retention and deletion

Local practice information stays on your device until you delete your practice, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall SaySharp. Use the delete-practice control in Progress to remove saved practice recordings, self-checks and journal notes. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling also removes local preferences. These actions do not automatically delete information already sent to Google.

Analytics user-level and event-level information is retained according to the Google Analytics property’s configured retention settings; aggregated reporting can be retained longer. Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Support correspondence is kept as needed to address the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

For questions or requests about access, correction or deletion, contact the privacy email above. We will handle requests according to applicable law. Because the app does not have user accounts and we do not attach an email address to app analytics, we may be unable to associate a support inquiry with a particular installation or analytics record. We will explain any relevant limitations.

## Permissions and your choices

Microphone permission is used when you choose to record a practice answer. Notification permission lets SaySharp display optional practice reminders. You can turn reminders off in Settings and revoke either permission in Android settings. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera or precise device location.

The current release does not offer an in-app switch for Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics collection. Removing the app stops future app activity from generating new reports, although previously collected information remains subject to the retention practices above.

## Security

We use app-private device storage and Firebase’s network services to help protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect access to your device, and avoid including sensitive personal information in practice recordings or support messages.

## Children

SaySharp is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the privacy email above.
