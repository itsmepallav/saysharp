Less rambling. More impact.

Build sharper communication skills with SaySharp, a 14-day speaking practice programme for everyday conversations, meetings and discussions.

Know what you want to say, but lose your point halfway through? Practise turning scattered thoughts into clear answers, stronger reasons and more precise words.

SPEAK CLEARLY, ONE SKILL AT A TIME
Each chapter gives you one practical speaking skill. Lead with your point. Give a useful example. Cut repetition. Make your case in fewer words.

RECORD, LISTEN AND REFINE
Record a short answer, listen back and check one thing. Try again, then use the skill on a different question. Written examples and simple prompts help you get started.

HEAR YOUR OWN PROGRESS
Keep your starting recording and compare your attempts over time. Checkpoints on days 7 and 14 let you revisit a familiar question and tackle a fresh one. You decide what sounds clearer and what to work on next.

HOLD YOUR GROUND WITH RESPECT
Practise explaining trade-offs, disagreeing clearly, setting boundaries and handling follow-up questions. Build confidence for conversations where your opinion matters.

FIND WORDS THAT LAND
Practise useful expressions and precise language. Revisit phrases in later sessions so you can try recalling and using them yourself.

TAKE IT INTO REAL LIFE
Every chapter includes a small task for your next conversation. Save a personal reflection, review your recordings and celebrate completed milestones. Optional daily reminders help you return to practice.

YOUR 14-DAY SPEAKING CHALLENGE
Work towards cleaner answers, stronger arguments and a more articulate voice. Follow the chapters daily or complete the programme at your own pace.

All 14 sessions are included free. No account is required. Recordings and notes stay on your device; usage and crash diagnostics are collected through Firebase. Recordings are not backed up and are removed if you uninstall the app.
